How this is built

Who qualifies for a category comes from data: moves and abilities from PokéAPI's Champions learnsets, pooled across forms (so Indeedee counts the female's Follow Me), plus Mega abilities. Where PokéAPI has no Champions learnset yet, the card says which game stands in.

Role scores and notes are judgement, written in data/draft-guide.json. So is the handful of Power adjustments below. If your read of the meta differs, change the file and rerun. Value fits log(price) against Power across all 247 Pokémon and scores each on how far below the line it sits. Pick is 60% Power and 40% Value.
