The one hard rule

Every Pokémon has exactly one owner.

If you have Incineroar, nobody else in your league does. A Pokémon changes hands through the draft, the market, a listing, a trade or an event, and when two managers go for the same one at the same moment, exactly one of them gets it. Draft night is a race.

How a season works

  1. Join a league

    Sign up at pokeleague.duckdns.org, create a league or join one with its invite code, and design your club's crest. A league of one works too: the draft becomes picking your own squad.

  2. Draft

    6 rounds in snake order, randomised. You pay the shop price for every pick out of ₽300,000, and a single S-tier costs more than that. So early on the choice is a good squad or most of one star. Run out of money and you skip your remaining picks.

  3. Climb the ladder

    There are no league fixtures. You play Champions ranked doubles on your own, then report each match: won or lost, who you sent out, their KOs, who fainted, and your rank afterwards. Everyone starts at Poké Ball 4, and the table ranks the league by ladder position.

  4. Get paid for winning

    Money only comes from wins, and a win is worth more the higher you are. Streaks multiply it. Only the first 10 matches of a round pay, so grinding can't buy the market.

  5. Run the club

    Sign free agents, sell back to the market, put a Pokémon up on the board at your own price, or trade with another club. Set your starting six, and name a captain.

  6. Answer events

    Things go wrong at a club: a star sulks, a flight doesn't land, the coach decides it's STAB moves only. Most events go up on an event board where clubs bid what they'd want to be paid to take one on. The lowest bid wins the money and the problem. You can't report another match until you've answered.

  7. Start again

    When the season ends, everyone goes back to Poké Ball 4 and every Pokémon except each club's captain is sold back at its current value. Then there's a new draft. The captain is the one Pokémon you carry from season to season.

Money and value

Money is only for signing Pokémon. There are no wages and no upkeep, and nothing tops you up except winning.

Poké Ball ₽1,000 — +3% / -3%

Ultra Ball ₽10,000 +₽20,000 +5% / -2%
Master Ball ₽25,000 +₽20,000 +6% / -2%
Champion ₽100,000 +₽20,000 +6% / -2%
  • Streaks multiply wins: ×3 from the 3rd, ×5 from the 5th straight win. A loss pays nothing, and never costs you money.
  • Signing is a commitment. You pay the full shop price and the Pokémon is then worth 45% of it, which is what you'd get back for selling it. Buying just to sell at a profit loses money.
  • Value is discovered by playing. Every match a Pokémon plays moves its value. Winning low barely helps and losing low hurts; at the top it's the other way round. It never falls below ₽1,000.
  • Running out is allowed. Spend it all on one star and you'll play a small squad until the wins come in.
